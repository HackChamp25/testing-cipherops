About CipherOps

Post-quantum DNSSEC, measured.

CipherOps measures what Merkle Tree Ladder (MTL) mode changes for DNSSEC, against classical algorithms and plain SLH-DSA, on real servers.

Purpose

Post-quantum signatures are large, and DNS answers have to stay small. MTL signs a zone once and gives each record a short proof. CipherOps lets you see what that saves: signing time, packet size and cache use.

Vision

A practical, data-driven path to quantum-safe DNSSEC, grounded in how real-time DNS operations actually work.
